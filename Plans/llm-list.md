# Free Open-Weight LLM Models & Exact Versions for Roleplay

However, "free" to run depends on your hardware: smaller models (8B to 27B) can run locally on a consumer GPU, while massive models (70B to 123B) require high-end multi-GPU rigs, high-capacity Mac Studio setups, or paid cloud inference providers (like RunPod or Featherless).

# Completely Free & Uncensored Open-Source Image & Video Models

While commercial AI services heavily censor their platforms, open-source weights allow complete freedom. "Completely free" means you download the model files and run them entirely on your own hardware or use free community web spaces, with zero subscription fees, paywalls, or built-in censorship filters.

Here is the research-backed list of the top uncensored open-source image and video generation models available:

## 1. Uncensored Image Generation Models

* **Pony Diffusion V6 (XL)**
  * *Type:* Finetune of Stable Diffusion XL (SDXL)
  * *Details:* Widely considered the gold standard for unrestricted, stylized, and complex character composition. It has virtually no built-in safety filters and excels at precise pose control and anatomical nuance.
* **Flux.1 (Schnell & Dev)**
  * *Type:* Base open-weight model by Black Forest Labs
  * *Details:* Offers state-of-the-art text adherence, realistic textures, and legible text rendering. While the official weights are open, they do not have hardcoded refusals built into the model weights themselves, making them heavily customizable via prompt styling.
* **SDXL Uncensored / Custom Community Merges**
  * *Type:* Community fine-tunes
  * *Details:* Various community checkpoints built on top of SDXL that have had safety classifiers stripped out from the training pipeline, allowing full creative liberty.

---

## 2. Uncensored Video Generation Models

* **Wan2.1 (14B / 1.3B)**
  * *Type:* Open-source video generation model released by Alibaba
  * *Details:* Currently one of the most powerful open video generation frameworks. Because the weights are fully open-source, it contains no cloud-side safety filters, allowing it to generate motion, physics, and complex scenes locally.
* **HunyuanVideo**
  * *Type:* Open-source video foundation model by Tencent
  * *Details:* A massive open model capable of producing high-definition video clips from text prompts. Being fully open-weight, it runs locally without external moderation or censorship.
* **LTX-Video**
  * *Type:* Lightweight open-source video generation model
  * *Details:* Designed to be faster and less resource-intensive than larger video models, making it ideal for running locally on consumer hardware to generate short video clips without platform restrictions.
